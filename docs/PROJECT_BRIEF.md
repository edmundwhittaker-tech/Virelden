# Playable slice — 22 October 2026

The owner's next request expands the movement starter into a small deliberate-action RPG, inspired by exploration and equipment progression rather than reproducing another game's world or artwork.

## This milestone

One compact, connected underworks ward beneath an unnamed outer Plate station. Two starter loadouts share the existing ordinary full-plate knight art. Three weapons differ in reach, arc, windup, recovery, damage and stamina cost. Two human enemy roles and an armoured captain provide a short combat/exploration loop. Find equipment caches, open a physical shortcut, temper mundane weapons at a forge, and return to a checkpoint after defeat.

Atmospheric textured stone and props must maintain legible combat and accurate collision. Preserve closed helmets, no cloth, the existing isometric projection and six-direction idle/walk sprites. Procedural combat effects are not bespoke attack animation sheets.

## Canon boundary

Public campaign material informs monumental Imperial stone, Plate stations, underworks, guarded cargo and relic-seeking people. This specific ward, encounter, equipment statistics and checkpoint mechanics are prototype inventions, not new campaign canon. No private GM material is used. Ordinary starter plate is **not true Warplate**; the forge cannot create or improve that rare lost craft. No teleportation shortcut or invented Plate connection is introduced.

## Acceptance

Reproducible install, strict TypeScript build, deterministic rule tests and real-browser input checks. Verify movement, distinct weapons, stamina/roll timing, loot/equip/upgrades, death/retry, paused menus, focus cleanup, saves and confirmed reset. Inspect final textured screenshots. Report authored, locally verified, CI verified and publicly deployed separately.

No multiplayer, world-scale map, deep quest system, true Warplate, account service, audio or touch controls in this slice.
