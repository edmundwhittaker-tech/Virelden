## Host interface and coordinate boundaries

Ground-space inputs: enemy/target positions, home, profile radius/speed/activation/leash, navigation occupancy and sweep. Projected game-pixel inputs: source/art transforms, equipment polygons, target body geometry. Source manifest/alpha objects are immutable once supplied; canonical geometry is cached per frame/scale/reflection. The host supplies `project` and its inverse `unproject`, and the same facing selector as the eventual renderer. Do not interchange these coordinate systems or apply the isometric projection twice.

`target(tick)` supplies `{position, body, alive, protected}`. `body` is a readonly array of `{a:{x,y},b:{x,y},radius}` capsules, **foot-local projected pixels for the target's current actual pose**, excluding weapons, shield, shadow and ground marker. It is authoritative and may change every tick. `protected` means a noncombat/sanctuary-style exclusion that cancels engagement; **NEVER map ordinary roll or damage/hurt invulnerability to this flag**. Ordinary immunity is checked by the host hit-resolution path at `event.contactTick`; rejecting damage still consumes that attack contact without cancelling enemy animation/cooldown. Optional `shield` polygons are absolute projected geometry from the same target pose; omitted means no shield. Target body/shield snapshots are deep-copied so an in-place host update cannot rewrite the prior tick. There is no import of `HUMAN_BODY`, no fixed88px player capsule copied into the contract, and no dependency on the player rig implementation. The player geometry interface supports a future pose-aware body adapter; final player dimensions are not accepted yet.

The host must provide:

- `canOccupy(groundPoint, fullProfileRadius)` and `canSweep(from,to,fullProfileRadius)` using complete actual geometry. No hidden eight-probe approximation is supplied here. Account for floors, walls, props, closed/open gates/shortcuts and relevant dynamic bodies.
- `clearLine(groundFrom,groundTo)` for attack obstruction, separately from body occupancy.
- `equipmentClear(pose, polygons)` and `equipmentSweep(fromPose,toPose,fromPolygons,toPolygons)` for **all actual transformed equipment**, including the long trident and shield. These callbacks must conservatively test physical obstacles in projected space; reject uncertain clearance. They are mandatory, not permissive defaults.
- **Required `resolveTargetContact(input)`** receives previous/current exact incoming weapon polygons and previous/current absolute target body/shield snapshots, plus tick/attack. It returns `{kind:none|shield|body, toi:0..1}`. Only body contact emits damage; shield contact consumes this attack and optionally emits `onBlock`. This is an injection boundary, **not an implemented swept solver**. The shared solver must establish earliest physical contact and shield-wins-ties for BOTH enemy attacks and incoming player weapon→giant shield/body. Current tests validate callback plumbing/consumption, not true geometric shield/body interception order.
- `target(tick)`, `isSanctuary(point)`, geometry `revision`, and `onHit(event)`. Use tick-indexed read-only world input; feed damage back through the host's normal invulnerability/stamina/health rules. Advance geometry revision when gates/shortcuts/collision change.

A successful pose transition checks both final equipment and intervening clearance. Blocked recovery still spends its full simulation cooldown; it cannot restart early or increase attack rate. Each successful windup commitment independently reserves `attackReadyAtTick = start + windupTicks + activeTicks + recoveryTicks + 1`; for the maw start1 this is157, never156. Navigation retry/revision changes, body-obstruction handling, sanctuary/noncombat cancellation and stagger cannot clear that deadline. Invalid body placement counts down existing recovery/stagger or aborts windup/active into nondamaging recovery. The deadline gates only a new windup, not safe planning/repositioning. Explicit defeat cancels all later contacts/navigation. On obstruction, simulation cancels the attack without damage and retains its last checked visual pose rather than snapping a weapon through a wall. Consumers must render `enemyContractPose`, not reconstruct a separate frame from phase/time. Discrete original source frames are not silently interpolated. A dynamic obstacle appearing inside an already occupied body/gear position remains a host/world reconciliation problem; the helper fails closed for body occupancy and cannot make physically impossible placements valid.

## Timing and navigation boundaries

At120Hz, suggested PR7 art timings round up to integer ticks. These are **optional helper timings**, not changes to current game balance. Windup expiry selects/checks only the current active endpoint before emitting a hit; it NEVER submits the previous windup geometry to the damage resolver. At entry, both weapon snapshots and both target snapshots are the current active instant, `fromTick == toTick`, and accepted body/shield contact must have TOI0 (nonzero entry-contact TOI throws). No unrendered windup-to-contact interpolation can deal damage. The event carries that exact active pose. During active, target contact may occur later, but only once per attack. Ordinary `active-step` intervals resolve previous/current active snapshots over `[tick-1,tick]`, allowing TOI0..1. The final `active-exit` interval resolves interior TOI<1 before recovery and excludes its now-recovery endpoint TOI=1. Events expose the interval kind/fromTick/toTick/endExclusive, dispatch `tick`, and `contactTick = fromTick + (toTick-fromTick)*toi`; entry contactTick is exactly its dispatch tick. Environmental equipment-clearance sweeps may conservatively reject pose transitions but never count their windup motion as damaging contact. Stagger and sanctuary interrupt without a delayed hidden hit. The accumulator does not discard valid elapsed time; dt outside0..10s throws. Equivalent dt partitions produce identical results for identical fixed-tick world samples. **Returning the latest rendered player state for every catch-up tick does NOT establish whole-game dt invariance.** Host input must include tick-appropriate movement, roll-pose body geometry, noncombat-exclusion/death state and body/shield pose; the host also needs an ordinary roll/hurt-immunity timeline indexed by event.contactTick. Noncombat `protected` and ordinary damage immunity are distinct. It does not promise equivalence if a host samples input/render time differently across calls.

Search samples32 bearings and4px rings, bounded by actual active weapon extents plus the injected target-body extent (hard search cap1024px). Canonical per-facing/frame equipment geometry is cached; cheap source bounds reject misses before detailed contact and candidate ordering uses distance lower bounds. The runtime resumes at most64 expensive candidate checks per fixed tick, with at most8192 arithmetic queue entries; pending planning is distinct from unreachable. World-callback costs and all-map/multi-actor performance still require native measurement. Movement invalidation after8px can restart a long resumable plan repeatedly; moving-target pursuit is NOT established and is a mandatory preactivation case (slow/fast/circling targets, blocked corridor alternatives, and no starvation). Every accepted stance must physically contact, be clear-line legal, occupiable at full body radius, and have a swept route. Up to six one-waypoint lanes are considered per candidate. A moving target or geometry revision invalidates stale stance exclusions. Optional target `geometryRevision` invalidates shape/extent planning when proportions or collision class change. Before a completed stance is accepted, current target contact/facing, occupancy, clear-line and the full route are revalidated; a cached old-pose best cannot be used blindly. Four blocked movement attempts enter cooldown instead of locking the nearest stance forever. This is **bounded local stance/lane search, not complete pathfinding**: legitimate routes can be missed by angular/radial sampling, geometry/floor seams, conservative callbacks or the limited detour set. It never compensates with teleportation, reduced collision radius or invisible reach.
