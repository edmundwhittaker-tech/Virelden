# Ground registration corrective pass

The existing 640-unit square remains the only playable area. A small inaccessible, connected masonry apron supports the sealed vault at the rear; it does not add another room or level. The offboard background is nearly black, with a continuous edged stone foundation instead of a void ellipse. The slate-court image covers the main court once; a continuous cropped material patch covers the rear apron. Neither is tiled as if seamless.

- `core/scenery.ts` registers actual world feet, collision radii, asset widths and ground origins. Solid coffers, forge, lamps, lever and rubble now block their footprints. Interaction distance remains unchanged; all interaction approaches are tested reachable from spawn.
- Four pillars use the same60px width and original aspect ratio (~223px high). Their ground centres coincide with the existing world collision circles. Unfooted offworld pillars were removed.
- Wall segments use their exact collision rectangles, articulated into short masonry blocks. Their face geometry follows the projection, unlike the previous stretched low-wall art. Original stone material is projected onto those exact faces and baked once into Canvas textures, preserving textured detail without runtime masks or shaders.
- Every tall prop sorts by its registered projected ground-foot Y, never its top or hardcoded screen position. Fractional stable slots break equal-Y ties. Short wall segments sort individually rather than treating a long wall as a single near-edge depth.
- Only geometry actually in front of the player's/another living actor's feet fades when its projected silhouette obscures that actor. Rendering does not promote the actor above a foreground pillar.
- Held weapons belong to the knight's local foot-depth rig; projectile depths follow their feet; world levers use their own world foot. Floor telegraphs, rings and shadows stay under solids. Labels and health bars are intentionally UI overlays, not physical geometry.

`?debug=1` exposes copies of registrations in `scenery` for independent browser assertions, with no state mutation API. Actual screenshot evidence is generated by the CI browser suite. See `KNIGHT_ATTACK_RIG.md` for the separate body/weapon contract.
